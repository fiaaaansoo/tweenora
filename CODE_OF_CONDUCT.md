# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(version 2.1, CC BY 4.0 license), summarized and adapted to this project.

## Our pledge

Taking part in Tweenora must remain a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, background,
gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, religion or orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and factual; criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback openly and give it constructively.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Think of the community as a whole.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexual language or imagery, personal attacks, insults, repeated provocation.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information (address, email, identity) without their consent.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers remove, edit or reject comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that do not comply with this code,
and may temporarily or permanently restrict their author's access to the project.

## Reporting a problem

Use GitHub's "Report content" feature on the content concerned, or open an issue at
https://github.com/fiaaaansoo/tweenora/issues without sensitive details, asking for a private conversation. Reports are
handled discreetly; maintainers do not disclose the identity of the person who reports.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and to people who represent the
project publicly.
